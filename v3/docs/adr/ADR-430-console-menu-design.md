# ADR 430 (amendments): The main menu design, as it changed on 2026 10 03

Status: Accepted (ships in ruflo-console 0.26.0)

Companion to ADR 430 (the menu's accents, chips and badges); both files carry the number 430. Extends: ADR 407 (the cockpit). Superseded in part by: ADR 442 (the Plugins page sits in TOOLS, not NETWORK).

## Amendment (2026 10 03): narrow panes keep their styling, and the groups fold

- **Cards and the nav card down to 44 columns, in a short pane too.** They were drawn only from 60 columns and never in a compact (short inline) pane, so a smaller pane fell back to the flat tab rows with the style buttons and lost the accents. They now draw from 44 columns (`MIN_CARD_COLUMNS`), compact or not. Under 44 the one-line nav is a solid chip in the page's group accent. The palette strip stays in a short pane.
- **Compact lays out like wide.** The title leads and the tabs follow (they were first). The menu wraps its mission-stage line into lines instead of cutting it at `SOP…`, and its status bar drops the modem text, then clips, before it would pass the edge.
- **The menu's groups fold.** Each group's bar has a ▾ / ▸ that hides or shows its entries (a folded group is its bar and an "N entries · ▸ opens" line); a row above the groups opens or closes them all. On a wide pane every group starts open; on a narrow one (a single tall column) only the first does. Specs: no text or row wider than the pane at any width from 30 to 120; every stage readable at every width; the fold defaults and toggles; the nav fits at every width from 44.

## Amendment (2026 10 03): fewer, meaningful sub-sections, and scroll to top

- **Sub-sections that earn their rule.** The five cards had 13 sub-sections, several of one entry that only restated it (`mission` → Missions, `protect` → Security, `build` → Dev Tools). They are now 9: SWARM *start here* (Missions, Overview, Swarm Topology) · *coordinate* · *observe*; INTELLIGENCE *learn* · *data & spend*; NETWORK & EXTEND *network* · *plugins*; and SAFETY & OPS and TOOLS (Settings, the AI terminal, the palette, help, log off) each a single section, which draws no rule at all. Every page is listed once. Specs: each page appears once (and Overview, Missions, Swarm and Settings are there); a card with several sections has two or more entries in each; the rules drawn equal the multi-section cards' sections; a card keeps its height while the entry animation plays.
- **Scroll to top.** Switching pages, opening help and opening the palette scroll the pane back to its first row (`$.ui.scroll({ to: 'start' })`, once at once and once more 80 ms later, so a page taller than the last still lands at its top). The terminal page's own wheel handling ignores that scroll. Pressing the page you are on does not scroll.

## Amendment (2026 10 03, later): the pages are spread across the sections, and the menu's top area stops repeating them

- **Balanced sections.** SWARM held nine pages over two rows while SAFETY and TOOLS held two each. Every page still exists, now spread: SWARM (Missions, Overview, Swarm, Hive-Mind, Claims, Approvals) · MIND (Learning, Neural, MetaHarness, Self-Evolution, Memory, Vector) · SAFETY (Security & Doctor, Cost, Performance, Timeline, Events) · NETWORK (Federation, x.ruv.io, Plugins, Skills, Plugin Catalog) · TOOLS (Terminal, Automation, Dev Tools, Settings). The menu's cards use the same assignment (a spec requires each page's menu colour to equal its nav colour). No group is more than one row, and a row too wide for its pane wraps onto the next instead of running through the card's border (the open page is counted at its full spelled name).
- **No repeated page row on the main menu.** The nav card at the top of the main menu is the groups and the search; the cards below already list every page. Picking a group chip shows that group's pages. Every page's key still works from the menu (the pages are hidden buttons).
- **The logo banner leads the menu in a small pane too** (it had been replaced by the big-letter page title in the compact layout), and its title keeps `AGENT SWARM CONSOLE` whole, dropping the version before it would cut the title.

## Amendment (2026 10 03, later still): a picked group on the menu no longer sticks

On the main menu the nav card shows only the groups until one is picked, and a pick was never cleared, so once a group chip was clicked its row of pages came back every time the menu was shown. Now: leaving a page resets the pick (the menu is idle each time you return); picking the picked group again closes its row, and a ✕ beside the chips does the same. On other pages a pick behaves as before (the shown group is the page's own). Specs: the pick is gone after leaving and returning; the second pick closes it and another group opens its own; no toggle off on another page; the ✕ is there only while a group is picked.

## Amendment (2026 10 03, last): icons beside the host's close mark

The ✕ in a pane's top right is Claude Code's own close mark: a mod cannot add to it (`$.ui.open` takes an id, title, focus, rows and columns; the `Pane` component's props are read-only). So the console draws a row of five small icons on the first line of its own body, at the right beside the ✕, with two cells of space after the last so it does not touch the close mark, next to the page's header art and purpose line: **⌂** the main menu, **⌘** the palette, **ʔ** help (the guide for the page; a small question-mark shape, since a plain `?` draws too large and ⓘ was not obvious), **⛭** settings, and **⟳** refresh last, beside the ✕ (the footer's Refresh: re-reads everything and replays the intro; a slightly larger glyph than ↻). From 110 columns each icon also spells its word (`⌂ menu`, `ʔ help` …), since a bare glyph is not obvious, help least of all; narrower, a glyph and a cell.

None of the glyphs is an emoji or has an emoji form (`?` drew visibly larger than the small symbols; `⚙` and `ℹ` are also emoji, which a terminal can draw two cells wide, notably in the bold lit state, making the row jump). The icon for the page you are on (the menu, the palette or help while open, settings) is lit: bold coloured text of exactly the width of a button (a plain Button draws exactly its label and ignores the primary style), so nothing moves when the page changes; refresh is an action and is never lit. The row is left out of a pane under 40 columns. Specs: the one-line row with its trailing space beside the header rows in four layouts; the words from 110 columns and none below; every icon does its one thing; the lit icon is the width of the button at 140 and 90 columns; no emoji glyph; refresh last; removing the wrapper or a glyph rule fails them.

## Amendment (2026 10 03, recorded from a live pane): found by watching the walkthrough

Recording the walkthrough from the real pane found what the specs could not: the page title art (a Raster drawn padded to 120 cells) ran under the icon row on wide titles, pushing out `⟳ refresh` and hiding `⌘ palette`; the boot's scan grid clipped names mid-word (`Self-Evolu`, `Plugins &`); the palette opened with four red `n/a` entries and a `▶` best match Enter could not run; and the Cost guide's tip had lost a `$` (`$$` collapsed to `$` by a string replace). Fixes: the header pictures are built to the width they use and never wider than the pane leaves beside the icons (`hooks/icon-layout.ts`), and the icon row spells its words only when the pane is wide and the art leaves room for the whole row; the scan grid shows short names (Harness, Evolution, Plugins, Catalog, Cost, Perf, Terminal); entries that cannot run sort after those that can, so the `▶` is something Enter runs; the tip is restored and a spec guards it.

The console's smoke contract (step 5) now names its one network exception: the update check's single GET (`$.http.fetch` in `register.ts`, used by `update-flow.ts` alone, ADR-429), and fails on any other. `controller.ts` is back under 500 lines (the drill-down log read moved to `drill-logs.ts`).

## Amendment (2026 10 03, later): a narrow pane, seen at phone width

The tour GIF has to read on a phone, where GitHub shrinks it to the screen width, so it is recorded from the narrow cockpit (60 columns) and the narrow layout was looked at as a phone reader would see it. Fixes from that: the icon row keeps four cells of slack and does not shrink in the host's flex layout (a lit icon collapsed to nothing and the last word wrapped when it was sized to the exact column count); the banner shows the longest title that fits (version, `AGENT SWARM CONSOLE`, `SWARM CONSOLE`, `CONSOLE`) and clips a long project name with an ellipsis; a page title that will not fit beside the icons drops the `ruflo |` prefix, then uses the page's short name, instead of being clipped mid-letter (`RUFLO SW`); the main menu's chip in a narrow nav says `MENU`, not `MAIN M…`; the welcome strip's network links wrap; the ruHelp field's label is just `›` when narrow.
